# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want `payment` to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming project for
contributors and users.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on code, observable behavior, and project goals.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about problems.
- Respect differing experience levels and technical perspectives.
- Keep discussions relevant to the issue or contribution.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or abusive language.
- Discriminatory comments or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions.
- Personal attacks in reviews, issues, or other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, reject
contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project or its
community.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
